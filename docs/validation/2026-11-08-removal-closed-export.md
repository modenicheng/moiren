## Archive and local acknowledgment

`closed-export.ps1` reads the original seal and captures the whole closed run,
including failed/empty evidence and directories, plus the immutable original
input/source/result provenance. The five staged scripts must retain their
creation-time pins. Probe/OBS executable trees are never copied. Limits are
eight path components,512total ZIP members,16MiB per file and64MiB uncompressed
total. Reparse paths, executable images, unknown artifact classes, missing
terminal pins and changed membership refuse without truncation.

Create-new sibling ZIP and inventory sidecar receive independent membership,
length and SHA256 readback. Local acknowledgment repeats that readback and
binds the original export reference, nonce, result, inventory and ZIP. Its
explicit meaning is `LOCAL_ARCHIVE_VERIFICATION_ONLY`; Transferred,
NativePassed and Qualified remain false. Failed readback preserves partial
outputs and prevents re-export/overwrite rather than manufacturing success.

Actual remote download, destination readback/acknowledgment, authorized cleanup
and reviewed native release are separate remaining integration work. Local
acknowledgment is not remote delivery or permission to delete a target run.
